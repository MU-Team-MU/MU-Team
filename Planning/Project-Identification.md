# 📋 Project Identification

---

### 📌 **Project Name:**
> **Travel Flights Booking System**

---

### ⚠️ **Current Problems:**
1. High Error Rate in Manual Data Entry
2. Operational Delays
3. Vulnerability to Data Loss and Damage
4. Wasting Time and Effort
5. Lack of Accuracy

---

### 📝 **Description of Project:**
> The system aims to create a comprehensive qualitative shift by automating all daily operations and procedures instead of traditional and manual methods. The system relies on a secure, centralized digital database that ensures records and data are stored with high accuracy while preventing any possibility of damage or loss, along with providing periodic backup capabilities. Furthermore, the system provides an organized and fast working environment that seamlessly connects different departments, significantly reducing human errors and speeding up task execution with high efficiency. In addition, the system is characterized by its ability to generate instant, accurate, and constantly updated reports, giving management a comprehensive and clear vision to assist decision-makers in making precise and effective decisions based on reliable data.
